Refund & Cancellation Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with Jeenie

📅 Effective Date: March 1, 2026  |  Last Updated: March 1, 2026

This Refund and Cancellation Policy governs all purchases made on jeenie.koax.in or via payment links sent to you on WhatsApp by Jeenie. All payments are processed through Razorpay, India's trusted payment gateway.

1. 💰 Credit Pack Purchases

1.1 Purchased Credits are Non-Refundable including:

  • Free credits provided as a signup bonus (10 free credits)
  • Referral bonus credits
  • Credits that have already been consumed/used
  • Any credit pack purchased more than 30 days ago
  • Credits purchased at a discount during promotional offers (unless otherwise stated)

2. ❌ Subscription Cancellations

Jeenie does not operate on a subscription model. You only pay for credit packs when you choose to. There are no recurring auto-payments or subscriptions to cancel. Each credit pack purchase is a one-time transaction.

3. ⚠️ Technical Failure Refunds

If you are charged but did not receive your credits due to a technical error (payment successful but credits not added), we will manually add the credits to your account

Please contact us immediately at support@jeenie.koax.in with your Razorpay Payment ID.

4. 🛑 Refunds for Terminated Accounts

If your account is terminated due to violation of our Terms of Service, you will not be eligible for a refund of purchased credits. If your account is terminated by us without cause, we will issue a full refund of unused purchased credits.

5. 🏦 Razorpay Dispute Resolution

All payments are processed through Razorpay. If you raise a dispute directly with your bank or card issuer (chargeback), we will cooperate fully with the bank's investigation. However, if a chargeback is raised fraudulently or against our Refund Policy, your account may be suspended and the credits forfeited.

We encourage you to contact us first before initiating a chargeback, as we process all valid refund requests promptly.

6. 📬 Contact

For all queries:

  • Email: support@jeenie.koax.in
  • Contact Form: jeenie.koax.in/contact
  • Response Time: Within 24 hours on business days (Mon–Sat)
  • Business Hours: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM IST
