Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

📅 Effective Date: March 1, 2026  |  Last Updated: March 1, 2026

Welcome to Jeenie ("Jeenie", "we", "our", or "us"), operated by Koax Industries OPC Pvt. Ltd. and accessible at jeenie.koax.in. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information about you when you use our WhatsApp-based JEE preparation service ("Service").

By using our Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Service.

1. 📥 Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • WhatsApp Phone Number: When you message Jeenie on WhatsApp, your WhatsApp phone number is automatically shared with us. This is your primary account identifier.
  • Messages & Queries: The content of messages you send to Jeenie, including text questions, doubt queries, and test requests.
  • Photos/Images: If you use the image doubt-solving feature, the photos you send to the bot.
  • Payment Information: When you purchase credits, payment details are collected by our payment processor, Razorpay. We do not store your card details. We only receive a transaction ID and payment status.
  • Contact Information: If you contact our support team, you may provide your name, email address, or other contact details.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Which features you use, how many credits you consume, timestamps of interactions.
  • Test Performance Data: Your answers, scores, and topics to generate your personalized weak-topic analysis and streak data.
  • Device Information: Limited device metadata provided by the WhatsApp Business API.

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Razorpay: We receive payment confirmation data (transaction ID, amount, status) from Razorpay when you make a purchase.
  • WhatsApp Business API: We use the official Meta WhatsApp Business API to operate the bot. Meta's own privacy policy governs their data collection.

2. 🎯 How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Jeenie JEE preparation service
  • Personalize your experience (weak topic tracking, tailored daily questions)
  • Manage your credit balance and process transactions
  • Operate the Daily Streak and Leaderboard features
  • Enable the Friend Challenge and Referral features
  • Send you service-related notifications within WhatsApp (e.g., streak reminders, credit balance low alerts)
  • Respond to your support queries
  • Detect and prevent fraud or misuse
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Analyze aggregate usage patterns to improve our AI models and question quality

🔒 We do not sell your personal information to third parties, ever.

3. 📤 Information Sharing and Disclosure

We share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We share data with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate the Service (e.g., Razorpay for payments, cloud hosting providers, AI API providers for answering doubt queries)
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government authority
  • Business Transfers: If Jeenie is acquired or merges with another company, your information may be transferred. We will notify you via WhatsApp before such a transfer
  • With Your Consent: For any other purpose, with your explicit consent

4. 🗂️ Data Retention

We retain your data as follows:

  • Account Data (phone number, credit balance): As long as your account is active
  • Chat Messages & Doubt Images: 90 days after the interaction, then automatically deleted
  • Test Performance Data: Up to 2 years, to enable historical progress tracking
  • Payment Records: 7 years, as required by Indian tax law
  • Support Communications: 2 years

You may request deletion of your account data at any time (see Section 7).

5. 🔐 Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2+
  • Sensitive data is encrypted at rest
  • Payment processing is handled entirely by Razorpay (PCI DSS Level 1 compliant)
  • Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • Regular security audits are conducted
  • Photos sent for doubt solving are processed and then deleted immediately after the response is generated

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. 👶 Children's Privacy

The Jeenie Service is intended for users who are at least 13 years of age. JEE aspirants are typically between 15–20 years old. If you are under 18, please ensure a parent or guardian is aware that you are using the Service and has agreed to this Privacy Policy on your behalf.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected such data, we will delete it immediately.

7. ✅ Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data by messaging Jeenie "delete my account" or contacting support
  • Opt-Out of Notifications: You can opt out of streak reminder messages by messaging "stop reminders" to Jeenie
  • Data Portability: Request an export of your test history and performance data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@jeenie.koax.in or via the contact form at jeenie.koax.in/contact.

8. 🍪 Cookies & Tracking (Website Only)

Our website (jeenie.koax.in) uses minimal cookies only for:

  • Session management (keeping you logged in)
  • Analytics (Google Analytics, anonymous) to improve the site

We do not use tracking cookies for advertising. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

9. 🔗 Third-Party Links

The Service may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., educational resources, Razorpay payment page). This Privacy Policy does not apply to those websites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

10. 📝 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When we make significant changes, we will notify you via WhatsApp message and update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the Service after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
